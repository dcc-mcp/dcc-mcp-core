Third-party component notice

This release contains third-party components from the Rust, Python, and npm
ecosystems. Their package names, versions, SPDX license expressions, and
upstream source references are recorded by the SPDX dependency inventory gate.

The release workflow validates that this notice is present in every published
wheel, source distribution, and standalone binary bundle. The SPDX inventory
is generated from the locked dependency metadata at release time.
