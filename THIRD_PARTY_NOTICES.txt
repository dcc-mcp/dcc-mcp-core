Third-party component notice

This release contains third-party components from the Rust, Python, and npm
ecosystems. Their package names, versions, SPDX license expressions, and
upstream source references are recorded by the SPDX dependency inventory gate.

The release workflow validates that this notice and the generated
THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt bundle are present in every published wheel, source
distribution, and standalone binary bundle. The SPDX inventory and license
texts are generated from the locked dependency metadata at release time.
